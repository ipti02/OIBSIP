Research Work

2025

Multilingual Text Summarization Using NLP

Thesis

Worked with multilingual political datasets (English, Bangla, Banglish), including real and synthetic data. Addressed challenges such as data inconsistency, code-mixed text processing, and redundant summaries. Developed a preprocessing pipeline for cleaning, normalization, and tokenization, followed by WGSS-based clustering for redundancy reduction and the use of Transformer models to generate accurate and fluent summaries.
